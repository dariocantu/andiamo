Andiamo Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Andiamo is a trip and event planner for families and friends, for iPhone and iPad. It is built so that your trips stay yours: there are no accounts to create, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run any server and does not receive, see or sell any of your data.

Where your data is stored

Everything you add to Andiamo — trips, events, itineraries, bookings and their documents, places you pin on maps, lists, receipts, photos, journal notes, check-ins, comments and reactions — is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit), so it syncs between your devices and survives reinstalling the app. Your name, settings and the people you've added are also kept in your iCloud account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with other people

When you share a trip or an event, the people you invite can see its contents, and the people traveling or going can edit it. Your first name is shown to them with what you add (for example "Added by Anna"). Sharing happens through Apple's iCloud sharing; you decide who is invited and can remove people at any time. People you invite to your Family see the trips you share with the Family; events are private unless you share them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Contacts — only when you search for or pick people to add. The name, phone number and email of the people you choose are saved in the app so you can invite them. Their phone number or email is used to look up whether they have an iCloud account for sharing (done by Apple) and to prepare the invitation message you send yourself. Nothing else from your contacts is read or stored.
  • Location — only when you check in. Your position at that moment is saved with the check-in and shared with the people on that trip. Andiamo does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos and camera — only the photos you pick or take are added to a trip. If you turn on "Save Camera Photos to Photos", receipts you photograph are also saved to your photo library.
  • Notifications — for reminders you set on to-dos, and to tell you when someone posts photos, check-ins or comments on a shared trip. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Services the app contacts

To make trips look and work better, Andiamo sends only a trip's or event's name, its place name or its map coordinates — never your name or other personal details — to:

  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, to find a freely licensed cover photo of the place or event (the photographer is credited in the app);
  • Open-Meteo, for the weather forecast;
  • Apple Maps, to search for places you choose, show maps and find the address of a check-in.

Some buttons open other websites in your browser only when you tap them: FlightAware (with the flight number, to track a flight), Google Maps and Google Calendar or Outlook (with the trip, event or booking you chose, including its place, times, notes and confirmation number, so they appear in your calendar), and links you saved yourself. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleted trips go to Recently Deleted and are removed for good after 30 days (or right away if you delete them there). You can leave a trip someone shared with you at any time. To remove everything Andiamo stored in iCloud, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Andiamo → Delete Data, then delete the app.

Children

Andiamo is not directed at children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
